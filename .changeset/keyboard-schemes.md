Studio: keyboard shortcut schemes. Settings › Keyboard shortcuts lists every editor action in tabs (Timeline, Player, Basic, Other) and lets you switch between OpenFilm's keys, Premiere Pro's and Final Cut Pro X's. Three custom schemes take any keys you record: changing a key in a built-in scheme copies it to a custom one first, and a key already in use asks before it moves. Menus and tooltips show the keys of the scheme in use.
